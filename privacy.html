Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Porchlight Ledger (“we”, “us”) runs porchlightledger.com. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Email signups

If you sign up for the free Debt Snowball Worksheet, we collect your email address. Signups are handled by our email provider, Kit (ConvertKit), which stores your address and sends our emails. We use it to send the worksheet and occasional budgeting tips and shop news. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to delete your address at any time.

Purchases

Our planners are sold on Etsy. We never see your payment details. Etsy's own privacy policy covers your purchase, and we receive only the order details Etsy shares with sellers, which we use to fulfill and support your order.

This website

This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Links to other sites

We link to Etsy, Kit, Instagram, TikTok and X. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers above, as needed to run the shop, or when required by law.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: hello@porchlightledger.com.
